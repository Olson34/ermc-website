## Transport

ERMC 2027 takes place in Katowice, Poland.\
Katowice has an airport located outside the city with connections to destinations
across the continent. Kraków Airport is around 2 hours away by train and 1 hour away
by car and has even more connections. A train from Warsaw to Katowice takes 3–4
hours. The city is also directly connected by rail to several other countries, including
Germany, the Czech Republic, Austria and Hungary.

## Venue
ERMC 2027 takes place in the [Katowice
International Conference Centre](https://www.mckkatowice.pl/en/), which is located in the city centre and forms part of a complex of buildings that includes the iconic Spodek landmark. A 400 m² ballroom will be our playing area with a separate room as the designated break area.
We have already received a very good offer from the venue and have tentatively
reserved the rooms for the championship. For a look at the venue, see the [video from
our previous tournament](https://youtu.be/Ma-TdKdO4vk). You can also view the [venue's official presentation](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1watHY8fHnY8-5AWhyQ0Af7S10uo0CiJD/view?usp=sharing) for much more in-depth information.
